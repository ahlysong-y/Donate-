Questions

Good to know

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. GiveHope is a registered 501(c)(3) organization (EIN 12-3456789). You'll receive a receipt by email immediately, and a consolidated statement every January for your tax filing.

How much of my gift actually reaches the field?

91 cents of every dollar goes directly to programs. The remaining 9% covers essential operations, audits, and payment processing fees.

Can I cancel a monthly donation?

Absolutely, anytime. There's a one-click cancel link in every receipt email, or email us at hello@givehope.org and we'll handle it within one business day.

Can I donate by bank transfer?

Yes — select "Bank" under payment method and our exact account name, number, routing, and SWIFT details will appear.

Can I share a video message?

Yes. Paste a YouTube link in the donation form and a preview will appear instantly.
